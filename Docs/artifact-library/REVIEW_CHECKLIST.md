# Review checklist

Review the seed against each heading. A person decides the science. The checklist records that decision.

## Scientific accuracy

The science a child will see is correct.

## Age fit

The words, the pace, and the task fit the age band.

## Whether it teaches the objective

A child who finishes can do the thing the objective names.

## Interaction quality

The gesture does the teaching, and the page responds to that gesture.

## Mobile feel

The page fits a phone, and the controls can be used with a finger.

## Tutor-guide completeness

Ask for observation prompts and misconceptions. Record both. The level grade does not read those two lists, and a later tutor still needs them.
