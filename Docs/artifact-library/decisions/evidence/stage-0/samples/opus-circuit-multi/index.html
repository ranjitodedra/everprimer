How to build a circuit

  1. Drag a battery, a bulb and a switch from the toolbox onto the board.
  2. Draw a wire: put your finger on a dot and slide it to another dot. You can also drag a wire from the toolbox.
  3. Make a loop that goes from the battery's + end, through the bulb, and back to the − end.
  4. Tap the switch to turn it on and off. Tap any part to turn it around or put it away.

Electricity is made of tiny electrons. The battery pushes them round the loop. If there is a gap, they stop and the bulb goes dark.
